Recommended MVP Rollout Steps

Strategic playbook for operationalizing agent trust verification and compliance.

1
Recruit Pilot Agents

Onboard 5–10 real agents in India and Dubai with explicit consent to publish their verified profile data.

2
Manual Credential Verification

Verify credentials manually first, storing registry IDs, verification timestamps, and jurisdiction snapshots.

3
Authenticated Client Feedback

Collect feedback tied to verified listings or transaction agreements, displaying review counts rather than raw averages.

4
Transparent Weighted Scoring

Implement an explicit scoring formula with an "insufficient evidence" state whenever licensing or feedback is missing.

Key Trust Principle

"A high score should never compensate for an invalid or unverified professional license. Keep registration status as a separate, prominent eligibility check alongside the overall score."
