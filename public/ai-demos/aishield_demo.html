Valuation Engine & Strategic Framework

How MillionFlatsShield™ computes transparent benchmarks and mitigates speculative asking bias.

Multivariate Base Rate & Multipliers

Base locality rates are anchored to verified transaction clusters. The engine applies transparent multipliers for property typology (Apartment: 1.0x, Villa: 1.15x, Penthouse: 1.25x) and status (Under-construction premium: +4% vs Ready resale). Outliers exceeding ±2.5 standard deviations are automatically excluded.

Ground Truth vs. Asking Price Separation

Unlike portals that average inflated asking listings, MillionFlatsShield maintains strict architectural separation between seller asking feeds and actual Maharashtra IGR registry sale deeds, preventing artificial price escalation across MillionFlats listings.
