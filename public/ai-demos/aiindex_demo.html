How the MVP Calculates the Score

MillionFlats AIIndex™ combines five weighted criteria to yield a balanced, explainable composite index score. Each pillar is assigned a fixed weight reflecting historical risk and yield drivers.

Category Weight Evidence to Use in Production
Location & connectivity 25% Google Maps distances, transit hubs, schools, and local amenities
Growth potential 25% Infrastructure pipeline, upcoming supply, historical price trajectory
Rental potential 20% Comparable rental yields, historical occupancy rates, net cash-flow yield
Developer credibility 15% Delivery track record, escrow compliance, regulatory disclosures
Risk & value 15% Comparable sales deeds, title encumbrance check, liquidity indicators
Scoring Mathematical Formula
Index Score = (Location × 0.25) + (Growth × 0.25) + (Rental × 0.20) + (Developer × 0.15) + (Risk × 0.15)

Default Evaluation: (85 × 0.25) + (78 × 0.25) + (74 × 0.20) + (88 × 0.15) + (72 × 0.15) = 21.25 + 19.50 + 14.80 + 13.20 + 10.80 = 80.0 / 100

Suggested Production Architecture

Integrate the Google Maps Platform for radius search and transit access, Walk Score where coverage and commercial licensing permit, institutional land records and rental benchmarks (e.g., Propstack, REIDIN, DLD) for financial metrics, and fine-tuned LLMs strictly for synthesizing multi-variable qualitative reasoning—never as the primary source of factual real-estate numbers.
